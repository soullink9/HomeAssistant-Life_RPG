# Code of Conduct

Life RPG is a small hobby project, and everyone who opens an issue, asks a question, sends a pull request or helps
another player should be able to do so without being made to feel unwelcome. This page says what that means here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people asking for help have already tried a few things.
- Stay on topic and be patient with questions, including beginner ones.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This breaks when the sensor is unavailable" is useful; "this is
  garbage" isn't.
- Respect that this is maintained in spare time. A slow reply isn't a no, and a no can be the right answer for
  the project.
- When you're wrong, say so and move on. That goes for the maintainer too.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, name-calling, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, age, body, ability or
  experience.
- Harassment of any kind, including repeated unwanted contact or following someone across issues or other sites.
- Sexual language or imagery, and violent threats or language.
- Sharing anyone's private information (addresses, emails, health data, screenshots of their Home Assistant)
  without their permission. Health data comes up naturally in a project like this; treat other people's numbers
  as private.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Where this applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki and code review. It also applies when someone is
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer, [@soullink9](https://github.com/soullink9). Include 
links to what happened if you can. Reports are kept private, and the person who reported won't be named without 
their permission.

You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option (the ⋯ menu on any comment), which goes to GitHub's staff.

## What happens next

The maintainer will look at every report and decide what's fair, which may be:

1. **A private note** explaining what was out of line.
2. **A warning**, with the comment edited, hidden or locked.
3. **A temporary block** from the repository.
4. **A permanent block** for serious or repeated behaviour.

Serious cases, such as threats or harassment, can go straight to a block.
